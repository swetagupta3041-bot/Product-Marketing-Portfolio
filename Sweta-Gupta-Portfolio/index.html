CASE STUDY 01 · THALIA TECHNOLOGIES

Make the product experience
part of the marketing.

A product-marketing practice for a portfolio of Shopify apps: understand the product, spot merchant friction, propose useful improvements, and carry the story through the lifecycle.

ROLEProduct Marketing Manager
PERIODDec 2025 — Present
PORTFOLIOShopify applications
THE CHALLENGE

Merchants do not experience a feature list. They experience every step.

I reviewed 15 Shopify apps to understand how product value was communicated and where setup, usability, retention, and discoverability could improve. The focus was to connect product detail with practical merchant needs, not add settings for their own sake.

60%Faster time-to-publish reported for company sites and landing pages
17App listings optimized, as recorded in my CV
15Apps included in the product and experience review
30+SEO blog posts enabled by the structured production workflow

The 60% figure is a CV-reported workflow result, not a measured uplift in app revenue or merchant conversion. The 15-app product review and 17-app listing optimization refer to separate scopes.

OUTLINK UX REVIEW

For Outlink, I developed an implementation-ready improvement specification, including:

01 · First-run setupA Shopify Admin-style home, merchant greeting, clear value description, a four-step setup guide, progress states, theme-extension status, and links to help and support.
02 · Product managementSearch by title or Shopify ID; filter by configuration and external-link status; select products for bulk actions; show setup state, link count, last-updated date, and clearly labeled row actions.
03 · External-link editingValidated URLs, long-link support, character guidance, test-link action, clear storefront visibility controls, logo settings, variant-level links, and a storefront preview.
04 · Safe savingA sticky save/discard bar, visible unsaved-change state, success feedback, inline validation, and confirmation before destructive deletion.
05 · Useful measurementA Performance Stats view for link views, clicks, click-through rate, date range, export, trend, and product-level results, with a helpful empty state before data exists.
06 · Plan-aware settingsBasic-plan feature information only where relevant; no unnecessary upgrade messaging for higher plans; clearer settings, helper text, accessible states, and support access.

My role was to analyze merchant needs, write and pitch product recommendations, and collaborate toward implementation. Individual recommendations should not be read as a claim that every item in this specification shipped.

LIFECYCLE & RETENTION

Keep the message useful after installation.

I planned lifecycle communication for the apps around the moments that matter: welcome, installation, payment, setup tasks, feature discovery, inactivity, uninstall, and the opportunity to return. Each app also needed timely feature-announcement emails, not only acquisition messaging.

Welcome→Install→Activate→Discover→Retain→Recover

The work included community participation in Shopify and LinkedIn, outreach to other app teams for in-app co-marketing, and weekly reporting across the app portfolio.

APP ECOSYSTEM & INTEGRATIONS

Find the next useful connection between Shopify apps.

I’m currently reaching out to complementary Shopify app teams to explore how our apps could work together. The goal is to connect products around a merchant workflow, so an integration solves a real task rather than existing as a logo swap or a feature checklist.

01 · MAP THE FIT

Look for adjacent merchant jobs, shared audiences, and workflow handoffs where connecting apps could remove a manual step or make an existing job easier.

02 · OPEN THE CONVERSATION

Approach relevant app teams, explain the customer use case, and learn about their product capabilities, integration model, audience, and partnership priorities.

03 · SCOPE THE EXPERIENCE

Shape an initial integration concept around the user journey: what triggers it, what information or action passes between apps, what the merchant sees, and how success could be measured.

04 · PLAN THE GO-TO-MARKET

Explore complementary in-app discovery and co-marketing, such as contextual app recommendations, feature education, or coordinated campaigns where the fit is useful to both customer bases.

In progressPartner discovery, conversations, and integration opportunities are being explored. No integration launch is claimed here.

My contribution is currently focused on identifying fit, initiating partner discussions, framing the merchant value, and connecting the opportunity to product and marketing follow-through. Technical feasibility and launch depend on agreement with each app team.

FREELANCE WORK · SEO EXECUTION

Independent work, embedded with the team.

Hands-on search strategy and implementation for a growing Shopify business.

FREELANCE · HEAVN MEDIA × KICKS MACHINE

Grow search visibility.
Stay close to conversion.

End-to-end SEO execution for Heavn Media’s Kicks Machine engagement, pairing scaled delivery with a commercial reality: more visibility only matters when the landing page and purchase path convert.

ROLEFreelance SEO & Growth Owner
SCOPEEnd-to-end organic search
PLATFORMShopify · India
THE DIAGNOSIS

Rankings were moving in the right direction. The business problem had shifted.

The project record showed strong Search Console momentum from April to July 2026. At the same time, organic sessions were broadly flat year over year while the recorded organic conversion rate declined from 0.73% in August 2025 to 0.14% in July 2026. Organic Search revenue moved from ₹38.60L to ₹10.78L across those two comparison months.

That gap changed the emphasis: keep strengthening discovery, while prioritizing commercial landing pages, merchandising, and the path from search intent to purchase. Ranking improvement is not the same as conversion recovery.

03

Created and published India-focused sneaker content, authentication material, trust pages, size-chart resources, and category-specific editorial plans.

04

Clicks were reported up 62% across two months. Growth came from categories including Fitbit/smart bands, Nike Air Yeezy 2 Red October, Timberland, Trapstar, activewear, and new collection pages.

What I would do nextPrioritize high-impression pages with weak commercial yield: clarify the offer, improve merchandising and internal paths, test page intent against paid-search learnings, and instrument conversion events before calling the work a revenue win.

The project record notes that some shopping-revenue growth was distorted by a few very large orders. I therefore keep Search Console visibility, Organic Search revenue, Shopping revenue, and conversion as separate signals.

I treat those as prepared work, not completed live fixes.
